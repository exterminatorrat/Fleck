general right to present a modified product as Fleck or imply endorsement by
the Fleck project.

No statement in this document limits rights that the MPL grants in covered
source files. In particular,
`Sources/FleckAgentBridge/FleckMCPBranding.swift` remains an MPL-covered mixed
source file even though it contains encoded Fleck mark data. Reusing or

## Reserved identifiers

The following identifiers are reserved for the official project and builds:

- the name **Fleck** when used as the name of a notes application or related
  service;
- the Fleck four-tile mark and logos; and
- confusingly similar names or marks that imply an official or endorsed build.

These marks are not represented as registered trademarks.

You may make truthful, reasonable references to Fleck, including statements
that a fork is based on Fleck. Do not describe a modified build as official,
use the Fleck marks as its primary branding, or imply sponsorship or
endorsement without separate permission. A fork should use its own name,
bundle identifier, application icon, and primary visual identity.
