# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use a Mac throughout the day and want to capture, clean up, and
organize thoughts into local notes without leaving the application they are
working in. They use Fleck repeatedly through keyboard and pointer shortcuts,
so the interface must remain immediate, unobtrusive, and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Fleck is a native, local-first macOS notes workspace. Its global dictation flow
captures speech, optionally polishes it, organizes Smart Capture into an
appropriate note, and saves locally with truthful progress and recovery. Success
means the user can speak naturally, understand what Fleck is doing at a glance,
and trust that content was not exposed or lost.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and quietly distinctive. Fleck should feel native to macOS and
recognizably its own, with visual confidence coming from its four-tile mark and
violet accent rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic system-blue status pills that could belong to any utility.
- Large transcription windows, dashboards, or transcript previews around a
  frequent capture action.
- Decorative glass, glow, gradients, bounce, shimmer, idle pulse, or success
  celebration.
- Copies of Wispr Flow or Superwhisper branding, artwork, colors, or exact
  silhouettes.
- UI that implies cleanup, routing, or persistence before the underlying state
  is authoritative.

## Design Principles

- Keep the frequent path immediate and nearly invisible until it has useful
  state to communicate.
- Use the Fleck mark as functional pipeline language, not decoration.
- Show truthful state and durable recovery instead of optimistic theater.
- Preserve the user's current application focus and local-first privacy model.
- Prefer familiar macOS behavior, restrained motion, and compact density over
  novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support VoiceOver, non-color state distinctions, Reduce Motion, Reduce
Transparency, Increase Contrast, keyboard alternatives, readable contrast, and
pointer pass-through outside the visible rail. Never depend on animation,
hover, or color alone to communicate or operate a state.
